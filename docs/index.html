How it works

From raw trade flows to an entry decision

Define the case

Product (HS6 code), target market, the company's home country and its size.

Pull official data

Import flows and supplier shares from Eurostat and UN Comtrade; macro, logistics and governance indicators from the World Bank.

Analyse

Market size and growth, supplier concentration (HHI), a 0-10 attractiveness score, CAGE distance and an EU-27 country ranking.

Decide

Verdict, PESTEL, regulation checklist, risk matrix, entry-mode recommendation and a 90-day action plan.

Frameworks in code

International Business theory, made computable

◎

Attractiveness score

Five weighted sub-scores (size, growth, openness, institutions, logistics) with published formulas.

▦

HHI concentration

Herfindahl-Hirschman Index with DOJ/FTC bands quantifies dependency on dominant suppliers such as China.

⇆

CAGE distance

Ghemawat's cultural, administrative, geographic and economic distance between home and target.

OLI entry mode

Transaction-cost rules choose between exporting, distributors, e-commerce, JVs, subsidiaries and WFOEs.

Sector-specific political, legal and regulatory checklist for the EU and China, linked to official sources.

⌖

EU-27 ranking

Ranks every member state on demand, growth, purchasing power, logistics and institutions.

Methodology

Transparent by design

Every number traces back to an official source and every score has a published formula, so a reviewer can audit the result.

Attractiveness score (0-10)

ComponentWeightFormula
Market size25%2 × (log₁₀ imports − 6)
Growth25%5 + CAGR / 3
Competitive openness20%10 − HHI / 1,000
Institutional quality15%mean of 4 WGI indicators, rescaled
Logistics15%(LPI − 1) × 2.5

Verdict: Enter if the score is ≥ 6.5 with at most one high/high risk, Enter with caution if ≥ 5, otherwise Do not enter now.

Data & limitations

  • Data quality checks: small markets, HS-code changes and low base years are flagged, and the growth signal is shrunk when data are unreliable.
  • Tariffs: MFN applied rates from World Bank WITS (UNCTAD TRAINS); FTA preferences from a curated agreement list.
  • EU market size: extra-EU imports, Eurostat Comext DS-045409 (EUR).
  • China market size: China-reported imports, UN Comtrade, excluding re-imports (USD).
  • Country fundamentals: World Bank GDP, growth, inflation, LPI and governance indicators.
  • Imports proxy demand served from abroad, not total consumption.
  • Hubs such as the Netherlands and Belgium re-export, which inflates their demand.
  • Regulatory notes are curated and must be verified on the official sources linked in each report.
Validation

Does the EU-27 ranking work out of sample?

Each member state was ranked with data up to t₀ only, then compared with what actually happened over the next four years. A score is only useful if it is tested.
